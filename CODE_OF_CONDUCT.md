# Our community agreement

UniWay is for people with different backgrounds, languages and abilities. Help make the project a place where someone can ask a beginner question without being embarrassed.

Be respectful, give specific and constructive feedback, and credit other people's work. Critique ideas and code rather than the person. Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, sexual harassment and publishing someone else's private information are not welcome.

Maintainers may remove harmful content or restrict participation to protect the community. Apply this agreement fairly to contributors and maintainers alike. For an ordinary concern, contact the maintainer through their GitHub profile without posting another person's private details. A confidential reporting address has not yet been configured; establishing one is a tracked pre-community-launch task.
